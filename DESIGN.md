# Design

## Creative Direction: Stardew Valley Pixel Art Aesthetic (Pink & Professional)

A bespoke, immersive portfolio inspired by the warm, nostalgic, and charming pixel UI of **Stardew Valley**, infused with a delicate pink and rose-gold palette. While visually playful and memorable, the content presentation maintains the highest standard of technical rigor, clarity, and professionalism for medical device and biotechnology employers.

## UI Architecture & Game World Parallels

1. **Top HUD & Status Bar:**
   - Permanent game header with Jalaica's character mini-avatar, level badge (`Lv. 26 Analytical Sciences Engineer`), and quick-access inventory tabs.
   - Live Stardew Clock (`Spring 13 · 10:00AM` / `10:00PM`) that toggles the Day/Night cycle between Sunny Blossom Farm and Starlight Dusk.
2. **Hero Dialogue Box:**
   - Authentic Stardew Valley NPC dialogue box framing Jalaica's custom pixel art portrait (biomedical engineer in lab coat with floral hair ribbon).
   - Dialogue choices serving as direct CTAs to inspect quests, open the skill inventory, or download the official 1-page PDF resume.
   - Player Stat Bars measuring compliance mastery, test method validation, and revenue recovery milestones.
3. **Career Quest Log (Work Experience):**
   - Formatted as the adventurer's Quest Journal.
   - Active Main Quest (SHL Medical: autoinjector stability, CCIT leak testing, $1M+ billing recovery) and Completed Quests (Integer QMS transition, InNeuroCo catheter validation, Prasad Lab biomaterials).
   - Explicit challenge statements, contributions, and unlocked rewards.
4. **Item Inventory & Skill Tree (Technical Skills):**
   - 4 Inventory Bags containing standards, testing methodologies, risk frameworks, and enterprise software as interactive inventory slots.
5. **Special Orders Bulletin Board (Projects & Leadership):**
   - Styled after the Pelican Town community board with pinned notices for the Hexaflow Needle ($60,000 sponsorship) and SWE/BMES STEM outreach (1,500+ students).
6. **Town Archives & Library (Education & Honors):**
   - Displaying Florida International University degree, Dean's List, OURS Scholar, and CURE Fellow medals.
7. **Farm Mailbox (Contact Section):**
   - Classic postbox and letter paper format with direct phone/email access and an interactive contact letter form.

## Color System

- **Day Mode (Springtime Farm):**
  - Sky/Background: `#ffeef2` with subtle pixel checker pattern
  - Wood & Frame Trim: `#4e2236`, `#853b5a`, `#c9658a`
  - Parchment Panels: `#fffbf5` and `#fff5ea`
  - Vibrant Rose: `#e11d48`, `#f43f5e`, `#fb7185`
  - Gold Trim: `#d97736`, `#f59e0b`
- **Night Mode (Starlight Dusk):**
  - Sky/Background: `#1a101f` with dark pixel grid
  - Wood & Frame Trim: `#120917`, `#4a2238`, `#803b60`
  - Parchment Panels: `#29182d` and `#341f3a`
  - Glowing Neon Rose: `#fb7185`, `#f43f5e`

## Typography

- Game Headings & Accents: `'Silkscreen'` and `'Press Start 2P'` (via Google Fonts).
- Body & Technical Descriptions: `'Plus Jakarta Sans'` for effortless readability by hiring managers and recruiters.
